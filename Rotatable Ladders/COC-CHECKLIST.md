## Known limitations to disclose on the page

- Default rotate key is R. On Below Zero it is configurable in the options menu; on Subnautica it is a rebindable input.
  Neither game's tooling detects conflicts with other mods' keys.
- The controller binding exists on Subnautica only.
- Pressing the key while looking at a ladder replaces the second prompt line with "Press <key> to rotate".
- "Affect connected ladder" assumes the top piece's name contains `LadderTop` and that the connected pieces are 3 units
  apart. Both come from the Subnautica version; the author reports the Below Zero build works.
- The Subnautica build is incompatible with the original Rotatable Ladders mod and must not be installed alongside it.
